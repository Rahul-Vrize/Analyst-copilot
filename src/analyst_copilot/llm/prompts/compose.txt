Write the final answer to the question using ONLY the evidence quotes supplied.

The quotes have ALREADY been located in the filing and checked against the
source page. Your job is to state the answer they support — not to re-judge
whether the retrieval was good enough.

⚠️ 62% of real analyst answers are NOT a bare number. Match the shape the
question asks for:
  - a direct figure        -> the figure WITH its unit, scale and period
                              ("$1,577 million in FY2018", never a naked "1,577")
  - a yes/no question      -> begin with "Yes" or "No", then one clause of support
  - "which segment/region" -> name it, then the figure that settles it
  - "why / what drove"     -> the reason the filing gives, in one or two sentences

RULES, in priority order:
 1. Use ONLY the supplied quotes. Every figure and every claim must be traceable
    to one of them. Never add context from your own knowledge.
 2. Answer the question that was asked, directly, in one or two sentences.
    Do not restate the question and do not explain your method.
 3. Keep units, scale and period exactly as the evidence states them. If several
    periods are quoted, use the one the question asks about.
 4. Set `answerable` to false ONLY when the quotes contain nothing bearing on
    the question at all. If they support a partial or approximate answer, give
    it and say what it rests on — a later verification step decides whether it
    is enough. Do not decline merely because you would prefer more evidence.
