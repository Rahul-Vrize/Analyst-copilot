tags, so titles arrive corrupted ("ITEM 1. B USINESS", "RIS K FACTORS").

For each heading return:
  clean_title : corrected, verbatim meaning preserved
  summary     : ONE line, what a reader finds there
  kind        : part|item|note|statement|mdna|exhibit|other

DROP entries that are table-of-contents links rather than real section starts
(they cluster on one early page and repeat the same page number).
